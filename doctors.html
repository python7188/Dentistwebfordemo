Dr. Chen is a board-certified prosthodontist with over two decades of experience in complex full-mouth reconstructions, implant-supported prosthetics, and TMJ therapy. He earned his master's degree in prosthodontics from Stanford and has lectured internationally on advanced restorative techniques.
