The Complete Guide to Daily Brushing

By Dr. S. Kumar · 1 March 2026 · 4 min read

Why Brushing Technique Matters

Most people brush their teeth twice a day, but few use the correct technique. According to dental research, proper brushing removes up to 30 percent more plaque than brushing with poor form. This means cleaner teeth, healthier gums, and fewer visits for restorative work.

The Modified Bass Method

The modified Bass technique is recommended by most dental associations worldwide. Follow these steps:

  1. Hold your brush at a 45-degree angle to your gum line.
  2. Use short, gentle, back-and-forth strokes across each tooth.
  3. Roll the brush head away from the gum line after several strokes.
  4. Brush the outer, inner, and chewing surfaces of every tooth.
  5. Tilt the brush vertically for the inner surfaces of your front teeth.
  6. Brush your tongue gently to remove bacteria.

How Long Should You Brush?

Aim for two full minutes, twice a day. Electric toothbrushes with built-in timers can help. Divide your mouth into four quadrants and spend 30 seconds on each.

Choosing the Right Brush

Use a soft-bristled brush with a small head that can reach all areas of your mouth. Replace your brush every three months, or sooner if the bristles are frayed. Both manual and electric brushes are effective when used correctly.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Brushing too hard — this can damage enamel and irritate gums.
  • Skipping the gum line — plaque builds up here first.
  • Using a hard-bristled brush — choose soft or extra-soft.
  • Rushing — two minutes is the minimum for thorough cleaning.
