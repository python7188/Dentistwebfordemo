5 Things to Know Before Getting Veneers

By Dr. N. Patel · 18 February 2026 · 5 min read

Porcelain veneers are thin shells bonded to the front surface of your teeth. They can correct discolouration, chips, gaps, and minor misalignment. Before you commit, here are five things every patient should understand.

1. Veneers Are Permanent

A thin layer of enamel must be removed prior to placement. This means the process is irreversible. Make sure you are fully committed before proceeding.

2. They Last 10–20 Years

High-quality porcelain veneers, when properly maintained, can last between 10 and 20 years. Avoid biting hard objects and maintain regular dental checkups.

3. The Process Takes Two to Three Visits

The first visit is a consultation and digital smile design. The second involves tooth preparation and temporary veneers. The third visit is for final bonding.

4. Not Everyone Is a Candidate

Patients with severe tooth decay, gum disease, or extensive grinding habits may need alternative treatments first. A thorough assessment is essential.

5. Cost Varies by Material and Complexity

Porcelain veneers cost more than composite but offer superior aesthetics and durability. Your dentist can provide a detailed treatment plan and payment options.
