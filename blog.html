5 Things to Know Before Getting Veneers

March 1, 2026 · By Dr. Jane Doe · 5 min read

Porcelain veneers are one of the most transformative cosmetic treatments available — but they're also one of the most misunderstood. Whether you've been thinking about veneers for years or just discovered them, here are five things every patient should know before committing.

1. Veneers Are Not One-Size-Fits-All

Every set of veneers is custom-designed to match your facial structure, gum line, and natural tooth color. Your dentist will work with you to choose the shape, shade, and translucency that looks most natural for your unique smile.

2. Minimal Tooth Preparation Is Required

A thin layer of enamel (about 0.5mm) is removed to make room for the veneer. This means the process is largely conservative compared to crowns. Some ultra-thin veneer systems require even less preparation.

3. They're Durable — But Not Indestructible

Modern porcelain veneers can last 10–20 years with proper care. Avoid biting on hard objects (ice, pens, fingernails) and wear a night guard if you grind your teeth. Treat them like your natural teeth and they'll reward you.

4. The Process Takes About Two Visits

The first visit involves preparation and impressions. Temporary veneers are placed while your permanent ones are crafted at the lab. The second visit is the exciting part — bonding day, where your new smile comes to life.

5. They Can Fix More Than You Think

Veneers can address chips, gaps, discoloration, minor misalignment, and even worn-down teeth. For many patients, they eliminate the need for multiple separate treatments.
