Permanent Solutions

Dental Implants

What it is: A biocompatible titanium root is surgically placed into the jawbone, then topped with a custom-crafted porcelain crown.

Who it's for: Patients missing one or more teeth who want a permanent alternative to bridges or dentures.

What it is: Clear aligners (Invisalign) and modern ceramic braces that gradually straighten your teeth with minimal visibility.

Benefits: Improved alignment, easier cleaning, reduced jaw strain, and a confidence-boosting smile — often in under 12 months.
