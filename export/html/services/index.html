Dental Implants

Overview

A dental implant is a titanium post surgically placed in the jawbone. It acts as a permanent root for a custom-made porcelain crown. Implants look, feel, and function like natural teeth.

How It Works

  1. Consultation and 3-D scan — we map your jaw and plan the implant position.
  2. Implant placement — the titanium post is set under local anaesthesia.
  3. Crown fitting — once healed, a custom crown is permanently attached.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. The crown may need replacement after 10–15 years.

Is the procedure painful?

The surgery is performed under local anaesthesia, so you feel no pain. Mild discomfort after the procedure is managed with medication.

What is the recovery timeline?

Healing takes 3–6 months as the implant fuses with the bone. A temporary crown is placed during this period.
